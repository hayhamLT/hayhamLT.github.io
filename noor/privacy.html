Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026. This covers Noor (a desk clock app) and Noor Remote (its iPhone app).

What Noor accesses

When you choose Connect Google in Noor Remote, you may grant read-only access to:

  • Gmail (read-only): the sender and subject of new messages in your Primary inbox, to show a notice and answer "any new emails?". Messages are read only when you ask.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): your upcoming events, to remind you before meetings.
  • YouTube (read-only): your subscriptions' new uploads, to tell you about new videos.

Noor never sends email, changes your calendar or acts on YouTube.

Where your data goes

  • Your Google access is stored only on Noor's device, encrypted with a key held in the device's secure hardware. It is not stored on the iPhone and not uploaded anywhere else.
  • Google data is fetched directly from Google to Noor's device. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.
  • When you ask Noor a question, the question and the minimum context needed to answer it may be sent to the AI service that writes her reply.
  • Camera and microphone processing happens on the device.

Your control

Disconnect anytime in Noor Remote (Controls → Accounts → Disconnect) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored access from Noor and revokes it with Google.

Noor's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
