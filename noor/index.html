Noor

A little AI creature living in a desk clock. She keeps the time, follows you with her eyes, plays the radio, answers questions out loud, and lets you know about new email, meetings and videos.

Noor Remote is her companion iPhone app: change her mood, talk to her, play music, set timers and connect accounts.

Noor is a personal project made for one desk; it isn't sold or offered as a service.
